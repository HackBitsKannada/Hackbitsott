See README "Getting started". Supabase, website, admin and Android release steps are added in Phase 9 after they are built and tested.

## Cost notes
- Supabase free tier: limited DB size, storage, bandwidth, and projects pause after inactivity.
- Cloudflare Pages: generous free tier, but not unlimited.
- **Video delivery is the expensive part.** Licensed HLS/DRM providers bill for storage and streaming (and DRM licenses). Free tiers do not cover real streaming at scale.
